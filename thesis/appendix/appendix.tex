\chapter{Implementation Notes}
\label{app:impl}

\paragraph{Hardware.} All experiments are performed on a single NVIDIA
A100 GPU. The replay buffers are GPU-resident; for the largest
configuration the flat buffer occupies approximately $1.5\,$GB.

\paragraph{Optimisers.} Adam with learning rate $3\!\times\!10^{-4}$
for all RL networks (actor, critic/value, temperature, goal-rep, flow),
and AdamW with learning rate $5\!\times\!10^{-5}$ for the world model.
Gradient clipping at $1.0$ is applied to every loss.

\paragraph{Target networks.} Polyak averaging with $\tau = 0.005$ is
applied to all critics, values, and the HIQL goal-rep $\varphi$. Target
updates are performed once per gradient step.

\paragraph{Batching and parallelism.} All RL methods use a batch size
of $256$ and $40$ gradient updates per outer iteration (reduced to $20$
in the dense reward setting). World-model roll-outs are vectorised
across $50$ parallel environments.

\paragraph{Constants.} $\gamma = 0.99$ throughout. The done-threshold
$\delta_d = 2.0$ and the dense-reward scale $\kappa = 1.6$ are
calibrated from the median single-step predictor drift in the LeWM
latent space, reported in \S\ref{sec:wm-drift}.

\chapter{WGSP Training Trajectory}
\label{app:wgsp-dynamics}

The WGSP method of \S\ref{sec:wgsp} failed by a specific
control-theoretic mechanism. The training trajectory of the headline
WGSP run is recorded in Table~\ref{tab:wgsp-dynamics} for reference.

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\caption{WGSP training dynamics ($224{\times}224$, advantage-J,
   $\beta_{\text{geom}}=0$, $N{=}8$, $M{=}4$, $k{=}3$, 500k total steps,
   warmup 10\%). \texttt{closer\_frac}: fraction of $N{\times}M$ WM
   rollouts where $\|z_k - g\| < \|z_0 - g\|$ (goal-directedness;
   $>0.5$ is better than random). \texttt{ll\_h}: LL HIQL log-prob loss
   (collapse $\to 0$). \texttt{ll\_d}: LL distil loss (negative
   $\Rightarrow$ runaway std collapse). \texttt{v\_std\_ep}: $V$ std
   across rollout endpoints ($J$ signal strength). Warmup ends at step
   50k; WGSP active from step 50k.}
\label{tab:wgsp-dynamics}
\begin{tabular}{rcccccc}
\toprule
\textbf{Step} & \textbf{Phase} & \textbf{closer\_frac} & \textbf{ll\_h} & \textbf{ll\_d} & \textbf{v\_std\_ep} & \textbf{J\_std} \\
\midrule
    0k & warmup & --   & 10.1 & --    &  0.0 &  0.0 \\
   50k & warmup$\to$WGSP & 0.505 & 272  & 0.00  &  7.2 & 32.7 \\

  100k & WGSP   & 0.584 & 181  & 1.39  & 18.4 & 49.8 \\

  200k & WGSP   & 0.578 & 86   & $-$0.19 & 29.4 & 57.2 \\

  338k & WGSP   & 0.564 & 9    & $-$3.15 & 30.8 & 55.7 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

\smallskip
\noindent\textbf{Diagnosis.} \texttt{closer\_frac} peaks at $0.588$
(step 150k) then regresses as the LL policy collapses:
\texttt{ll\_d} crosses zero at $\sim$195k, entering a runaway
positive-feedback loop (\texttt{ll\_h}: $272\to 9$, a $30\times$ drop
versus the LeJEPA baseline $\approx 187$). Root cause: the
distillation loss $-\mathbb{E}[w_{\text{HL}}\log\pi_{\text{LL}}]$ has
no lower bound on the Gaussian standard deviation, so minimising a
negative loss forces the LL towards a near-deterministic point,
collapsing WM rollout diversity and degrading the HL's learning
signal.
\end{table}
